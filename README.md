# 🔓 Smartest.bg Locked Exam Bypass

A simple step-by-step guide on how to exit and bypass the locked full-screen exam mode on [Smartest.bg](https://www.smartest.bg).

---

## 📋 Requirements

* A functioning brain with at least 10 brain cells 🧠
* Permission to install Chrome extensions
* A secondary account on your Chromebook *(optional)*

---

## 🚀 How to Do It

1. **Log in to your secondary account** *(optional, if you have one)*.
2. **Install the extension:** Add the [Always Active Window](https://chromewebstore.google.com/detail/always-active-window-alwa/ehllkhjndgnlokhomdlhgbineffifcbj) extension to your Chrome browser.
3. **Enable the extension:**
   * Go to [smartest.bg](https://www.smartest.bg).
   * Click the **Puzzle icon** (Extensions) in the top-right corner of Chrome.
   * Click on **Always Active Window**.
   * Open the extensions menu again to ensure the extension icon turns **blue** *(blue indicates it is active; if it is gray, click it to activate)*.
4. **Prepare the exam:**
   * Open your test link.
   * Double-check the extension icon to ensure it remains **blue**.
   * Enter your name and proceed to the next step.
5. **Bypass the lock:**
   * Locate the **"Започни теста"** (*Start Test*) button.
   * Click **"Започни теста"** and **immediately** press the `Esc` key on your keyboard. *(Timing is crucial—you need to be extremely fast!)*

---

🎉 **Done!** The exam environment should now be unlocked.
